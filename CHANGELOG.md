# Changelog

One line per change, newest first.

- 2026-11-17 20:48 · In a classroom, a subagent or lead that shows up walks in through the door and one that finishes walks out through it and is gone within 2.5 s; desks stay put.
- 2026-11-17 20:22 · Click a building to step into its classroom: you stand at the back as an observer, the project's leads stand at the front, their subagents sit at desks in their hats (a raised slip when one needs you, askew when stuck), open PRs sit on a table by the door, and a whiteboard shows the project's phase, its note and the workflow's steps (done, now, next, later) from `.claude/phase.json`'s new `steps` list. Escape or the back icon returns to the town; the work view is one icon away.
- 2026-11-17 19:00 · The work view keeps a session's terminal hidden until you press Show terminal (nothing renders or connects meanwhile), shows no command text on rows or panels, shows status as a coloured dot with its word instead of an emoji, and has a Close session button (`claude stop`: the conversation is kept and `claude attach` resumes it).
- 2026-11-17 16:08 · The desk queue puts sessions that ask a question or need a permission or plan approval ahead of ones that only finished a turn, in the town, the Needs you list and ⌥N; oldest first within each group.
- 2026-11-17 15:52 · The steward reviews a pull request only when you ask: a button on the town's parcel panel and on the work view. A reply that isn't a review no longer counts as one, and typing to the steward yourself stops further requests. Each review names its project, repo and PR.
- 2026-11-17 11:56 · The town shows only projects you added in Settings (and their worktrees) plus the steward: chats in other folders stay out of the town and Morning. PR boxes show only PRs you opened. A session keeps only its newest letter.
- 2026-11-17 11:11 · Funtown opens on a 3D clay town: your castle with you at the desk and the steward as queen, one building per project in its colour, each session standing at its building, its working agents as hats on a rack, open PRs as parcels; a session that needs you walks to your desk and queues, a stuck one sits down, finished ones leave. No words on the town: names show on hover, a click opens the session's panel, and Settings holds adding and removing projects, mute and "I had to switch". The list, panels and terminals moved to `/work.html`. New font: Recursive (OFL, bundled with its licence).
- 2026-11-17 08:44 · Projects and shells: add a project folder in the app (kept in `~/.funtown/projects.json`) and it shows even with no session; start a background run with a prompt in its own worktree; each project has a Shell tab with your login shell, which keeps running (a dev server goes on) until you close it. A new project shows how to accept Claude's trust prompt once.
- 2026-11-16 21:40 · Workflow view: a project's `.claude/phase.json` shows its phase, a note and tags; each subagent shows its role; open pull requests show as parcels (via `gh pr list`); two sessions editing the same file each get a warning. One steward session in `~/.funtown/steward` gets each ready PR once and its review letter shows on the parcel (`~/.funtown/steward.md` changes the request, empty turns it off).
- 2026-11-16 21:09 · A clay kit of parts for the coming 3D town (houses in project colours, the castle, characters with a hat per role, desk, parcel, ground), all drawn in code with an ink outline; see them at `/kit.html`. Browser tests take `FUNTOWN_E2E_PORT` (default 4799).
- 2026-11-16 19:20 · An "I had to switch" button notes when and why you left Funtown (`~/.funtown/switches.jsonl`). Hooks and the server share one error log (`~/.funtown/errors.log`) that names the failing step; a crash, a failed start and, on the next start, a run that ended without shutting down are logged. Also: the Morning window starts at its last successful load, the letter shape reaches resumed sessions, and hook paths with shell characters work.
- 2026-11-16 18:55 · The page loads faster: the letter renderer and the terminal load only when a panel shows them (first load 239 kB, was 905 kB).
- 2026-11-16 18:30 · Sessions get one short letter shape (Done, Next, Needs you) at their start, from `hooks/letter.md` (your own `~/.funtown/letter.md` overrides it). A Morning view shows per project each lead's newest letter, who got stuck and who waits; letters are kept in a local journal for 7 days and survive a restart. Subagent letters show their handback report.
