# Enter answers only low and medium cards, after a second on screen

The user's first words were "Enter takes the agent's pick". We narrowed that: Enter sends a card only
when it is armed (a low or medium question with a choice in every question, or a medium plan, the
only card in its pane, on screen for a second since it appeared, since focus left a text box or the
terminal, and since the window came to the front, and not a key repeat). A 1.5 s undo window
follows. A high card, a "not stated" card, a high plan and every permission are click only.

We weighed Enter on everything with a pick (with Enter twice on high cards) and Enter on everything
with the undo window as the only guard. A wrong Enter on a push or a deploy can't be walked back,
and a habit is hard to take away once it is learnt, while loosening the rule later is easy.

## Consequences

- Until agents write the level word, every question is "not stated" and Enter works on plans only.
- The arm rule is the only key path to an answer; card forms no longer send on Enter by themselves.
