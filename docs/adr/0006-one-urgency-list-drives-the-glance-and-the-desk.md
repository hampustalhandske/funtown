# One urgency list drives the Glance and the Desk

One pure function in the page orders everything that needs the user, in strict tiers: asks (cards
first, then other asks), then stuck sessions, then burning houses, then quiet things (stuck on a rate
limit, a PR waiting, resting leads), oldest first within each. Space walks it, the plain list shows
it, the castle queue follows it, alerts come only from its loud tiers, and the town's one loud thing
is its head.

We weighed fires first (GOAL.md's "what's burning" as the headline) and one list oldest first across
every kind. An ask or a stuck agent has stopped work and waits on the user; a red PR waits too but
costs nothing by the minute. A rate limit stops every session at once, so it is quiet rather than
loud.

## Consequences

- The Desk builds tiers 1, 2 and 4; "What the town knows" adds tier 3, burning houses, to the same
  function.
- A lead that only finished a turn is resting, last in the list and never loud.
